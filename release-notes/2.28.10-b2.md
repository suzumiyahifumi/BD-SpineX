# BD-SpineX 2.28.10-b2

> ## ✅ Built for BrownDust II `2.28.10`

The cleanest way yet to run your mods — with a fresh new look.

- **`LIBRARY`** — browse your mods as visual cartridges
- **`ROSTER`** — find any mod by character, no folder-digging
- **`PREVIEW`** — watch mods animate and compare two before installing
- **`THEMES`** — new print-style color themes; make it your own

---

**Get going:** close the game → **Install Runtime Injection** → pick mods → launch.
